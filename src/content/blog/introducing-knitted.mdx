For centuries, the art of tailoring has remained largely unchanged. The tools of the trade—shears, needles, chalk—are timeless. But the business of tailoring? That's a different story.

Managing clients, tracking measurements, scheduling fittings, and handling invoices often involves a chaotic mix of spreadsheets, paper notebooks, and WhatsApp messages.

**We built Knitted to change that.**

## What is Knitted?

Knitted is a purpose-built operating system for independent ateliers. It's designed to fade into the background, letting you focus on what you do best: creating beautiful garments.

> "A great tool doesn't ask for your attention. It gives you focus."

### Key Features

1.  **Client Profiles**: Store measurements, preferences, and order history in one secure place.
2.  **Order Tracking**: Visual status pipelines from "Fabric Sourcing" to "Final Fitting".
3.  **Automated Reminders**: Let customers know when to come in, automatically.

## Why Now?

The bespoke fashion industry is experiencing a renaissance. People crave connection, quality, and sustainability. They want clothes made *for* them, by people they know.

Knitted enables this renaissance by removing the administrative friction that holds small studios back.
